How to use

Application rates

Shake well, mix into water and apply as you normally water. Use it from germination to bloom, in soil, coco, soilless mixes or hydro.

The chart

Rates by growth stage

These are the rates we use in our own greenhouse. Because Greenfin is an amendment, not a concentrated fertilizer, it is gentle, but stick to the chart for the best results.

Greenfin application rates. Shake well before use.
StageRate
Germination1–2 ml per gallon of water
Cloning2–4 ml per gallon
Root dip50 ml per gallon
Plant growth6–10 ml per gallon on the first watering, then 3–6 ml per gallon for the rest of vegetative growth
Bloom3–6 ml per gallon
Golf & lawn12 oz per acre (1 gallon per 10 acres)

Quick converter

How much do I add?

1 US fl oz = 29.57 ml. 1 teaspoon is about 5 ml.

Good practice

Getting the most from living microbes

Shake, then mix

Shake the bottle well, measure, and stir it into your water. Apply right away while the microbes are lively.

Use it all season

Microbes keep working through the whole life cycle, so keep applying at your stage's rate from seed to harvest.

Brew a compost tea

Greenfin works well in compost teas, adding living biology to your brew.
