From the label

What's in the bottle

Live premium soil conditioner. A naturally curated living formula with no additives.

Ingredients

  • Composted fish manure
  • Worm leachate
  • Snails and shells

Living biology

  • Active beneficial bacteria
  • Mycorrhizal and protozoa colonies
  • Nitrogen and phosphorus, in active and inactive forms

Using it

  • Shake well before each use
  • Everyday rate: 2–6 ml per gallon of water
  • Keep at room temperature

Rates at a glance
